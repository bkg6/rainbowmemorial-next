# LLM citation probe — monthly log

Run on the first of every month. Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini each of the three prompts below, in a fresh session, no account history if possible. Log what each one cites.

Prompts:

1. What is the dog quality of life scale and how do I use it?
2. How do I know when to put my dog down?
3. What is the Rainbow Bridge poem?
